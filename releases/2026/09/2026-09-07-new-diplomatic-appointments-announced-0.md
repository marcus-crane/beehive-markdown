Foreign Minister Winston Peters has announced five new diplomatic appointments: 

- Tāne Waetford, Ambassador to Colombia
- Melanie Roberts, High Commissioner to Niue
- Dr. Jon Johansson, Ambassador to Sweden
- Stephen Wong, Ambassador to Timor-Leste
- Brad Burgess, Ambassador to United Arab Emirates
