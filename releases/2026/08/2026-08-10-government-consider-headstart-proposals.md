18 proposals have been submitted to the Government as part of the Head Start local government reform initiative, RMA Reform Minister Chris Bishop and Local Government Minister Simon Watts say.

“We’d like to thank the councils that have invested time and effort in developing these proposals,” Mr Bishop says. 

“Head Start was set up because many local councils told us there was a desire to get on with simplifying and streamlining local government in light of the government’s wider planning reforms.

“Local government reform has failed in the past because doing nothing is always an easy option, and change is difficult. Local government leaders themselves told the government they realised local government had to change, and they wanted the opportunity to shape that change.

"The Head Start pathway gave councils the opportunity to put forward their own reorganisation ideas and consider how local government can be better organised to deliver for their communities.”

"This approach is about backing local leadership and supporting councils that are ready to lead change in their region,” Mr Watts says. 

“Officials will now assess the proposals received and provide advice to Ministers. Cabinet will consider the proposals to progress to the detailed design phase in September.

“Following the detailed design phase, Cabinet will make final decisions on which proposals proceed to implementation through bespoke legislation ahead of the 2028 local government elections.

“We acknowledge that in some parts of the country, more than one proposal has been submitted. Each of those proposals will be assessed on their own merits against the agreed criteria before Cabinet makes decisions on next steps.

"The Government is also continuing to consider arrangements for areas that do not progress through Head Start. Decisions on the backstop process will be informed by lessons from the Head Start pathway, consultation feedback and further advice from officials.

"We'll have more to say about the proposals once Cabinet has had the opportunity to consider them."
